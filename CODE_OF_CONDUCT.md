# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using
welcoming language, respecting different viewpoints, accepting constructive
criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting comments,
publishing others' private information, or other conduct that could reasonably
be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the private channel described in
`SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do
not align with this Code of Conduct.

This text is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
